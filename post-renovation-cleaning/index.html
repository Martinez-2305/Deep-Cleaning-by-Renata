After-builders cleaning in Wimbledon

Building work leaves behind a fine dust that settles on every surface and keeps coming back. Renata clears it properly, so you can see the finished work and start using the space again.

Why building dust needs its own clean

Plastering, sanding and cutting make dust far finer than everyday household dust. It drifts into cupboards, onto door tops, along skirting boards and into light fittings, and it carries on settling for days after the work stops.

A quick dust or vacuum often just moves it around. That’s why an after-builders clean works from the top of each room down, collecting what falls on the way and finishing with the floors.

One clean or two, depending on the project

A smaller job may only need one final clean. On bigger projects, two visits can work better. Renata will suggest what suits yours once she knows what work was done.

  1. Stage 1 Once the trades have finished

    Builders’ clean

    The heavy dust-down: dust cleared from surfaces, fittings, skirting and floors, so you can see the finished work clearly and spot anything that needs snagging.

  2. Stage 2 Before furniture goes back or you move in

    Final clean

    The detailed clean: kitchen and bathrooms, internal glass where safe and accessible, and floors vacuumed and mopped, ready to live in or hand over.

For homeowners and for builders

  • Homeowners

    After an extension, loft conversion, refurbishment or new kitchen. Send a few photos and a note of what was done, and the quote will reflect how much dust and detail is really involved.

  • Builders and developers

    Renata also works directly for builders and developers handing a property back to a client. Timing is agreed around your programme, so the clean happens after the last trade and before handover.

Good to know before you book

  • Book once building work has finished and trades are off site, so dust doesn’t settle again.
  • Paint splashes and adhesive residue are assessed separately.
  • Heavy waste and rubble removal aren’t offered.
  • Work at heights and exterior windows aren’t offered.
How soon after the work finishes should I book?

As soon as the trades are off site. If anyone is still working, dust will settle again behind the clean.

Will I need one visit or two?

It depends on the size of the project and how much dust there is. Renata will advise once she has seen photos.

What do you need from me for a quote?

A short description of the work, the size of the space and a few photos. Photos can be sent by WhatsApp.

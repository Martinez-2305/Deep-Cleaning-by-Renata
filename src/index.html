How it works

There are no fixed prices, because no two properties are the same. The size of the property, its condition and what you need done all affect the quote.

Request a quote

  1. Send your enquiry

    Your postcode, the type of clean and a short description — number of rooms, and anything unusual like heavy limescale or building dust.

  2. Agree the scope and quote

    We confirm what’s included and the price. Nothing is booked until you’ve agreed both.

  3. The clean is completed

    On the date you’ve arranged, to the checklist you’ve agreed.

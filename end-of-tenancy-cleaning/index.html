Who books an end of tenancy clean

The standard is the same whoever books it. What changes is what you need from it.

  • Tenants moving out

    You want the property handed back in the condition your tenancy asks for. Share your check-in report or inventory if you have it, and the checklist is built around it.

  • Landlords between tenancies

    You need the property ready for the next tenant, and for photos or viewings. Tell Renata when the keys are free and when the next move-in is, and the clean is planned in between.

  • Letting agents

    You need one person to deal with, a clear price and the work done to the agreed scope. Access and key handover can be arranged directly with your office.

What’s cleaned, room by room

A guide to a typical end of tenancy checklist. Your quote confirms exactly what’s included for your property.

Kitchen

  • Worktops, splashbacks and tiles degreased
  • Cupboards and drawers cleaned inside and out, once emptied
  • Hob and appliance fronts
  • Sink, taps and plughole descaled

Bathrooms

  • Bath, shower, screen and tiles cleaned and descaled
  • Toilet cleaned and disinfected
  • Basin, taps and mirrors
  • Drain covers lifted and cleaned where accessible

Bedrooms, living areas and hallways

  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames
  • Light switches and sockets wiped
  • Accessible internal windows and sills
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Need more? Inside the oven, fridge or freezer cleaning, carpet cleaning and rubbish removal can be added, subject to availability and quoted separately.

The details that get checked

At checkout, attention often goes to the same places: grease in the kitchen, limescale in the bathroom, and dust along skirting boards and around switches. These are the areas a quick clean skips, so they get proper time here. This skirting board and switch are from one of Renata’s own jobs.

Getting ready for the clean

A few things that help the day go smoothly.

  1. Send the inventory

    If you have the check-in report or inventory, send it with your enquiry so the checklist can follow it.

  2. Empty the property first

    Cupboards, drawers and wardrobes can only be cleaned inside once they’re empty, so book the clean for after your things have gone.

  3. Keep the power and water on

    A thorough clean needs electricity and hot water, so check both are still connected on the day.

  4. Agree access

    You don’t need to be there, as long as access and key handover are arranged in advance.

Deposits and checkout

No cleaner can honestly promise your deposit back. Deposit decisions are made by your landlord or letting agent, and they cover damage and wear as well as cleaning. What Renata can do is clean properly to the checklist agreed with you.

What if the agent points something out after the clean?

Let Renata know as soon as you can, with a photo if possible. Anything raised is looked at case by case.

Is oven cleaning included?

Inside the oven is an extra, quoted separately, so mention it in your enquiry if your tenancy needs it. The hob and appliance fronts are part of the kitchen clean.

How is the price worked out?

Every clean is quoted individually, based on the size and condition of the property and what needs doing. You’ll know the scope and price before you book.

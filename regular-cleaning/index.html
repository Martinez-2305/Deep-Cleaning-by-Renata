A plan that fits your home

Before the first visit, you and Renata agree what matters most: the rooms that need attention every time, and the jobs that can be done less often.

The plan isn’t set in stone. If your routine or your priorities change, tell Renata and the plan can change with them.

For example, a plan might include

  • Kitchen worktops, hob and sink
  • Bathrooms and toilets
  • Dusting reachable surfaces
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Skirting boards or internal windows on alternate visits

Your own plan is agreed with you.

Starting with a deep clean

If your home hasn’t had a thorough clean for a while, a one-off deep clean first can bring it up to standard, so regular visits spend their time keeping it there. Renata can advise whether it’s worth it.

Is there a minimum visit length?

Yes, visits are at least 2 hours. Larger homes or more detailed plans may need longer, and your quote will say how long.

Do I need to be home?

No. You can be at home if you prefer, or arrange for Renata to hold a key.

Will it be the same cleaner every time?

Deep Cleaning by Renata is run by Renata herself, so the person who quotes for your home is the person who cleans it.

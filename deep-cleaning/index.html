One-off deep cleaning in Wimbledon

When a deep clean makes sense

  • Before selling or letting

    A clean home photographs better and feels better at viewings.

  • After moving in

    Start fresh in a home someone else has lived in.

  • When it’s overdue

    After a busy few months, an illness or a new baby, when the cleaning has slipped.

  • Before regular cleaning starts

    Bring the home up to standard, so weekly or fortnightly visits can keep it there.

  • Small commercial spaces

    Salons, small offices and short-let flats, as well as homes.

Where the time goes

Most of a deep clean is spent on a handful of jobs that build up slowly.

  • Grease in the kitchen

    Cooker hood filters, splashbacks and cupboard fronts. Filters are soaked and degreased, not just wiped over.

  • Limescale in the bathroom

    Shower trays, glass screens, taps and tiles, descaled with natural-based products.

  • Dust along the edges

    Skirting boards, door frames and switches, which gather the dust a vacuum passes over.

Inside-oven cleaning, fridge or freezer cleaning, carpet cleaning and rubbish removal can be added to a deep clean, subject to availability and quoted separately.

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property, so you’ll get a time estimate with your quote.

Do you bring your own products?

Yes. Renata brings her own products and equipment, using products made with naturally derived ingredients, chosen with care for homes with people and pets. Mention any allergies or sensitivities before the clean.

Can you deep clean a salon or small office?

Yes. Small commercial spaces such as salons, small offices and short-let flats can be deep cleaned too. Describe the space and how it’s used, and Renata will quote for it.
